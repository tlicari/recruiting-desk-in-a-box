# Outreach voice

Direct. Short. Specific. Partner, not vendor. Draft only; send only candidates the recruiter has named.

## Subject
Pattern `[Role] – [Geography]`, e.g. `Implementation Manager – California`. Give 3 options; default to the shortest true one.
Never: "Exciting opportunity", "I came across your profile", keyword-stuffed headlines.

## Open: four beats, two sentences max
1. **Who you are** — "I'm [Name], a recruiter working…"
2. **The seat** — role + geography.
3. **One true public fact** — exactly one, from their resume or public profile: years in territory, named employer + title, a real number, a certification, a product line.
4. **The ask** — a short call, or a reply to a few questions.

Template: `I'm [Name], a recruiter working the [role] in [geo]. Your [one fact] stood out. [Ask].`
Company one-liner only if they wouldn't know the firm. No brochure.

## Banned phrases
- I hope this email finds you well
- I came across your impressive background
- We're a dynamic, fast-growing…
- I've been following your career
- Your recent post
- Your reputation in the industry
- You came highly recommended
- I know you're busy, but
- I'd be happy to help
- Great question! (when nobody asked)
- I'll be honest with you
- Exciting opportunity

## Length
First touch 80–130 words, optional 6–8 short screening questions. Follow-ups shorter. LinkedIn note 50–80 words, draft only, never automated.

## Signature
Use `Templates/signature.md` verbatim. Never invent one.

## Self-check before handing over
Subject matches pattern · exactly one fact, and it's verifiable · no banned phrase · word count in range.
