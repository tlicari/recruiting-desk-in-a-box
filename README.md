# Recruiting Desk in a Box

**Short description:** Turn your agent into a recruiting desk partner. It cuts a raw candidate list in four passes, scores survivors on a 9-point evidence card, writes short outreach that sounds like a person, runs day-5 and day-10 follow-ups, and sorts every reply into a clear next step.

## Who it's for
Independent recruiters, boutique search firms, and in-house TA teams who source the names themselves and want a disciplined partner for the rest.

## Division of labor
- **You:** sourcing, choosing who gets contacted, submissions, interviews, offers, client relationships.
- **The desk:** cut, score, desk card, one-page packets, outreach drafts, follow-up, reply triage, tracker.

Nothing goes out until you name the candidates. The desk drafts; you approve.

## Skills
| Skill | What it does |
|---|---|
| `candidate-cut-passes` | Pass 1–4 cut order: knockouts → evidence check → score → gate |
| `desk-card-scoring` | 9-point evidence card, A/B/C tiers, desk card + forwardable packet |
| `outreach-voice` | Subject `Role – Geography`, 4-part open, one true fact, banned-phrase list |
| `follow-up-sweeps` | Day-5 and day-10 sweeps, then close the loop |
| `reply-classification` | Interested / Not-now / Info-sent / Referral / Unsubscribed / Wrong-person |

## Setup
1. Put your rubric in `Scorecard_active.md` (template in `desk-card-scoring`).
2. Put your signature in `Templates/signature.md`.
3. Hand the desk a list: LinkedIn URLs, resumes, or *First Last + company + target seat*.

## Principles
- Evidence beats adjectives. Numbers, dates, named employers, certifications.
- One rubric live at a time. Swap it per search.
- Unsubscribed and Rejected are terminal.
